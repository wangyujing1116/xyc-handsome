About me

I'm Yucheng Xing (邢宇程), a PhD student at the Saw Swee Hock School of Public Health, National University of Singapore, supervised by A/Prof. Mengling Feng.

My research focuses on uncertainty quantification and evidential deep learning for medical AI — in particular survival prediction from whole-slide pathology images and multimodal clinical data, with an emphasis on calibration, robustness under domain shift, and missing modalities.

Open to collaborations. Feel free to reach out if you are interested in my research!
